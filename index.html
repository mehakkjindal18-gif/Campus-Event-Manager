Can I use the portal on mobile? ＋
Yes. The layout collapses into a mobile-first navigation and keeps calendar, planner, attendance and registration actions within thumb reach.
How does Duty Leave fit into the planner? ＋
When an event overlaps a lecture, the planner marks the lecture as “Apply DL” and keeps the application state attached to that event.
Does the portal support reduced motion? ＋
Yes. prefers-reduced-motion is respected: cinematic transitions fall back to simple, accessible fades.
Where do certificates go? ＋
Completed event records can be stored in a searchable certificate history, keeping proof of participation in one place.
